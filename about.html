About

Our story.

One griddle, nine stools and a recipe we never changed.

Since 2014

It started with nine stools.

We opened a tiny counter with one flat-top and a burger we’d been making at home for years.

The line went round the block in a month. We still make it the same way.

We grind fresh chuck every morning. We smash it thin on a 450° griddle. We stack it on a bun toasted in butter. That’s the whole secret.

What we won’t change.

Never frozen.

Fresh chuck, ground every morning, used the same day.

Made to order.

Nothing waits under a lamp. Every burger is smashed when you order it.

Paid fairly.

Every crew member starts above the local living wage.
